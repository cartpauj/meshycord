Policy

What the bridge is allowed to create in Discord without being asked.

Safe: your node's channels are already yours, so there is no abuse surface here. Off by default: there are often dozens, which fills a server and hits Discord's 50-channels-per-category limit. The riskiest of the three: anyone who has heard your advert can send you a direct message. Even when this is on, it only fires for senders already in the radio's contact list — an unknown sender always goes to the inbox, which is the guard that makes the whole policy safe. A mesh message is 160 bytes; longer text is split into numbered transmissions. Anything needing more than this is refused rather than truncated, because a truncated message looks identical to one that sent. At {{.Cfg.MaxChunks}} that is about {{.ChunkCapacity}} characters of plain text. Applies to mesh channels only. Airtime is shared with everybody else on the mesh, and a group message cannot be acknowledged, so a pause is the only pacing available there. Direct messages and room posts pace themselves: each transmission waits for the previous one to be acknowledged before the next goes out. A group message cannot be acknowledged, so the only evidence it went anywhere is hearing a repeater pass it on. During this window the message shows ⏳; the moment a repeat is heard it becomes ✅, and if the window closes in silence it becomes 📡 — transmitted, but nobody in earshot was heard relaying it. A repeater waits a random delay of up to about 2.5× the message's airtime before rebroadcasting, and a second hop takes as long again, so eight seconds covers two hops comfortably. Waiting longer costs nothing but the hourglass; too short reports messages as unheard while they are still spreading. A room-server login does not expire: the room writes your permission into its client table and saves it to flash, so it survives reboots at both ends. What can end it is eviction — the table holds 20 clients, and a new login displaces the least recently active one. So this is how long to go without re-checking, not a session lifetime. 0 logs in before every message, which costs a full round trip over the air per post to re-prove something the room already has written down. A background job that logs in to each room server again on this interval, quietly. It keeps the bridge recently-active so a busy room evicts somebody else first, and it restores a session that was evicted before anyone types into it. 0 turns it off and logins happen only when a message needs one. 0 keeps everything. History lives on the SD card, so a bound is worth having.

Console login

This page holds your bot token and every room password. At least 8 characters. Changing it signs out every other session.

Danger zone

Reset deletes every channel the bridge created — all linked channels, the Channels / Room Servers / Companion DMs categories, and the inbox — and clears every link. Channels you made yourself are untouched.
